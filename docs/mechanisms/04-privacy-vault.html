The honest edges

It cannot find what nothing looks for. Structural patterns and host-declared values are exact. A name or a street address with neither, and no registered detector, crosses in the clear. The vault reports when it knows its own coverage was partial; it cannot report what nothing looked for.

The invariant is narrower than “only issued tokens resolve”. Because the body is error-correcting, strings within one symbol of an issued codeword also resolve, and those were never issued. What can be promised is that only exact codewords and that explicit radius resolve at all.

It protects the paths that go through it. Content sent to a provider without calling deidentify is not covered, in the same way the rest of the library governs the paths routed through it.

Mechanism only. deidentify, reidentify, prepare_tool_call, restore_policy and destination_policy are real. Token bodies and addresses are invented. No benchmark numbers appear, because numbers go stale and mechanisms do not.

Verified against GuardLLM 2.0.0, commit 4a8fefa. API names, policy fields and orderings can change too, not only numbers.
